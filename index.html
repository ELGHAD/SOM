Unveiling Moroccan Talent, Celebrating Culture

Sounds of Morocco is a 100% independent Moroccan electronic music label, supporting Moroccan DJs and producers as well as international talent. More than a label, it’s a cultural project led by producer Yas.Weeyzz, celebrating music, creativity, and the rich Moroccan heritage through innovative electronic productions and artistic collaborations, while helping artists release their music worldwide on major platforms.

Label & Distribution

Discover our independent label and digital distribution services for Moroccan and international electronic music artists. We help you release your tracks on Spotify, Apple Music, Beatport, YouTube, TikTok and more so your sound can travel far beyond Morocco.

Musical Cultural Project

Dive into our cultural project and join us on a musical journey across Morocco, fusing traditional sounds (gnawa, chaabi, amazigh rhythms and more) with modern electronic music. We highlight Moroccan identity, North African stories and underground scenes through curated releases, events and collaborations.
