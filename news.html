Industry & Scene Insights

Moroccan Electronic Music & Heritage

Sounds of Morocco aims to build a bridge between Moroccan heritage (Gnawa, Amazigh, Chaâbi, and regional folk traditions) and contemporary electronic music, through residencies, collaborations and long-term artistic development.

Our approach is to respect tradition while creating new narratives that speak to today’s audiences locally and globally.

Building a Platform for Independent Artists

As an independent label and cultural project, Sounds of Morocco supports emerging artists with distribution, creative direction and international connections, helping them navigate the music industry while staying true to their identity.

We work with producers, singers, instrumentalists and visual artists to design sustainable careers rooted in the Moroccan and North African ecosystem.

From Local Residency to Global Release

Each residency is designed as a complete cycle: research, creation, collaboration, live performance and finally digital release on major platforms (Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music, etc.).

This method allows us to document the process, involve local communities and transform each chapter into a cultural and musical statement from Morocco to the world.

More Residencies Across Morocco


Sounds of Morocco is expanding its cultural footprint with over 10 residencies across Morocco, including upcoming projects in Errachidia, Fès, Chefchaouen, and many more regions rich in heritage and musical identity.

Stay tuned for new chapters, collaborations, and cultural journeys.
