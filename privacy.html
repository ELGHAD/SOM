Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. Learn how we collect, use, and protect your data.

UPDATED: June 20, 2025

Sounds of Morocco (“Sounds of Morocco”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) cares about your privacy. This Privacy Policy (“Policy”) explains what personal information we collect when you use our services, how we collect it, how we use it, how we share it, and what rights you have in relation to your personal data.

This Policy applies when you access and/or use our website at soundsofmorocco.com, when you contact us by email or through our forms, when you submit demos or collaboration requests, when you subscribe to our newsletter, and when you use any other products or services we offer (collectively, the “Services”).

Please also review any Terms of Use or Cookie Policy we may make available, which govern the use of our Services and explain our use of cookies and similar technologies.

1. What Personal Information Does Sounds of Morocco Collect?

For the purposes of this Policy, “personal information” means any information about an individual from which that person can be identified, either directly or indirectly. Depending on how you use the Services, we may collect the following categories of personal information:

  • Contact details: name, artist name, email address, phone number, postal address.
  • Account information (if an account feature is available): username, password and other information used to secure your account.
  • Artist and demo information: demo links (e.g., streaming URLs), project name, social media profiles, biography, and any other information you choose to provide in our demo or collaboration forms.
  • Order and payment information (if we offer a store or ticketing): billing address, shipping address, contact details, and payment confirmation information (actual payment details are typically processed by third-party payment providers).
  • Technical and usage data: IP address, device type, operating system, browser type and version, pages you visit, time and date of your visit, actions taken on the site (clicks, scrolls, forms viewed), and other analytics data.
  • Communication data: messages you send us (e.g., questions, support requests, collaboration inquiries, press or partnership contacts).

If you choose not to provide certain information, we may not be able to offer you some of the Services or respond fully to your requests.

2. How Does Sounds of Morocco Collect Your Personal Information?

We collect personal information in different ways, including:

  • Directly from you: when you fill out a contact form, send a demo, subscribe to the newsletter, request information, make a purchase (if available), or otherwise communicate with us.
  • Automatically: when you browse the Site, we may collect technical and usage data using cookies, pixels, log files and similar tracking technologies.
  • From third parties: such as analytics providers, hosting providers, payment processors (if we sell products or tickets), or social media platforms, in accordance with their own privacy policies.

3. How and Why Does Sounds of Morocco Use Your Personal Information?

We use your personal information for various legitimate business purposes, including:

  • To provide and operate the Services: to manage the website, display content, process demo submissions, respond to your requests, and, where applicable, process orders or reservations.
  • To communicate with you: to respond to your messages, send you important information about your interactions with us, or contact you about a demo or collaboration request.
  • For marketing purposes: to send you newsletters, updates on new releases, events, collaborations, and other communications that may be of interest to you, in accordance with your preferences.
  • To improve and customize the Services: to analyze how visitors use the Site and to help us design a better user experience, content structure and navigation.
  • For security and fraud prevention: to protect the Site and Services, detect and prevent fraudulent or abusive activity, and ensure the integrity of our systems.
  • To comply with legal obligations: to meet any applicable legal, regulatory or contractual requirements and to protect our rights and the rights of others.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Like many websites, we use cookies and similar technologies (such as pixels and tags) to operate and improve the Site. Cookies are small text files stored on your device that help us remember your preferences and understand how you interact with our pages.

We generally use cookies for the following purposes:

  • Essential cookies: to enable core website functionality.
  • Analytics cookies: to measure traffic and usage patterns to improve the Site.
  • Preference cookies: to remember your language or other settings.

You can usually configure your browser to refuse cookies or to alert you when cookies are being sent. If you disable cookies, some parts of the Site may not function properly.

5. Your Choices and Opt-Out Options

You have certain choices regarding how we use your personal information, especially for marketing:

  • Email marketing: you can opt out of receiving promotional emails at any time by clicking the “unsubscribe” link in the email or by contacting us using the details below.
  • Cookies: you can manage or disable cookies in your browser settings, although this may affect the functionality of the Site.

Even if you opt out of marketing communications, we may still send you non-promotional messages, for example about important service updates or responses to your requests.

6. Links to Third-Party Websites and Services

The Site may contain links to third-party websites or embedded services, such as streaming platforms, ticketing services, or social media networks. These third parties have their own privacy policies and we are not responsible for their content, practices, or how they handle personal information.

We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party site or service before providing them with your personal information.

7. Data Retention

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this Policy, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law (for example, tax, accounting, or legal requirements).

When we no longer need to use your personal information, we will delete it or anonymize it so that it can no longer be used to identify you.

8. International Data Transfers

Sounds of Morocco may operate or use service providers located in countries other than your own. As a result, your personal information may be transferred to and processed in countries that may have different data protection laws than those in your jurisdiction.

Where required by applicable law, we will take appropriate measures to ensure that any international transfers of personal information provide an adequate level of protection, such as using contractual protections or other safeguards.

9. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the personal information we hold from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is completely secure.

While we strive to protect your personal information, we cannot guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for keeping any account credentials confidential and for notifying us promptly if you suspect any unauthorized access to your account.

10. Children's Privacy

Our Services are not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so that we can delete the information.

11. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technologies, legal requirements, or for other reasons. When we do, we will update the "UPDATED" date at the top of this page.

We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed about how we handle personal information.

12. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your country or region of residence and applicable law, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information, which may include:

  • The right to access the personal information we hold about you;
  • The right to request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information;
  • The right to request deletion of your personal information in certain circumstances;
  • The right to object to or restrict certain types of processing;
  • The right to withdraw consent where processing is based on consent;
  • The right to request a copy of your data in a portable format, where applicable.

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details in the “Questions or Comments” section below. We may ask you for additional information to verify your identity before fulfilling your request.
